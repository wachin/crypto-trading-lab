# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Crypto Trading Lab exists to give ordinary people an honest research
instrument instead of a hype machine. Everyone is welcome here —
beginner or expert, writing code by hand or with an AI agent — and we
pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- **Critique the work, not the person.** A wrong number, a weak test or
  a vague requirement is fair game; the contributor is not.
- **Be honest and precise.** Say what you ran and what it printed. "It
  should work" is not a result. If you do not know, say so.
- **No profit promises.** This project never guarantees returns, and no
  contribution may imply that a backtest predicts the future.
- **Respect the safety rules.** Real trading stays disabled, strategies
  never bypass the risk manager, and no dependency is installed without
  the maintainer's approval (see [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md)).
- **Assume good faith and explain kindly.** Many contributors are
  learning; a clear "here is why" beats a terse "you are wrong".
- **Keep it on topic.** Questions, ideas and research results belong in
  Discussions; reproducible bugs belong in Issues.

## What is unacceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Discriminatory jokes or slurs.
- Encouraging anyone to trade money they need to live, or presenting a
  speculative strategy as a safe investment.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space — Issues, pull requests,
Discussions, commit messages and code review — and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive or unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@wachin](https://github.com/wachin), using the same private channel
described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). All reports are reviewed and
handled confidentially. Maintainers may take any action they consider
appropriate, from a private correction to a temporary or permanent ban
from the project. Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith
face the same consequences as anyone else.
