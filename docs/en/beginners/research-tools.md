# Research Tools

This guide explains the research tools in Crypto Trading Lab.

## Research Notebook

The research notebook helps you document your experiments.

### How to use:

1. Click **Research → Research Notebook**
2. Enter your hypothesis
3. Document your process and observations
4. Save your entry

### Why it matters:

- Keeps your research organized
- Helps you learn from past experiments
- Required for reproducibility (Chapter 53)

## AI Research Assistant

The AI assistant helps answer research questions.

### How to use:

1. Click **Research → AI Assistant**
2. Select your research context
3. Type your question
4. Review the response

### What it can help with:

- Strategy development ideas
- Data analysis questions
- Performance evaluation
- Risk assessment
- Portfolio optimization

### Important notes:

- The assistant provides suggestions, not financial advice
- Always validate with out-of-sample testing
- Never trade money you cannot afford to lose

## Connecting Research to Trading

Your research should lead to qualified strategies before trading:

1. **Document** hypothesis in notebook
2. **Test** with backtesting (Chapter 37)
3. **Validate** with walk-forward analysis (Chapter 45)
4. **Qualify** with robustness tests (Chapter 44)
5. **Promote** through safety gates (Chapter 67)

Only after all steps pass should a strategy be promoted to paper or real trading.
