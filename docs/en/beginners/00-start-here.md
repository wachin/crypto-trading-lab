# Start Here: Cryptocurrency for Complete Beginners

Welcome to Crypto Trading Lab.

This guide is for you if you have never traded anything, never used an
exchange, and maybe never even held cryptocurrency. No prior knowledge
is assumed. Take your time — nothing here will ask you to risk money.

## What Crypto Trading Lab is

Crypto Trading Lab is a **research and practice workshop** for
cryptocurrency markets, built for learning. Inside it you can:

- look at real market history in chart form;
- build simple, rule-based trading strategies without writing code;
- test those strategies against past data (**backtesting**);
- practice with **paper trading** — simulated money, zero risk;
- measure results honestly, including all the costs and mistakes most
  beginners never notice;
- learn each concept step by step in the Learning Center.

Think of it as a **flight simulator** for trading. A flight simulator
does not make you a pilot, but it lets you make every beginner mistake
— safely — before you ever leave the ground.

## What Crypto Trading Lab is not

Being honest with you from the start is a core value of this project:

- **It is not a money-making machine.** No program can guarantee
  profits, and anyone who promises that is lying to you.
- **It is not a market predictor.** Nobody — no person, no software —
  can reliably predict where prices will go. The tool searches for a
  statistical edge and tells you honestly when there isn't one.
- **It is not financial advice.** It is a learning instrument.
- **A profitable backtest is not proof of future profits.** Past
  results do not guarantee anything about the future. This is one of
  the most important lessons you will learn here, and the application
  will keep reminding you of it.

## The farmer's truth

Here is the honest way to think about what this tool can do:

A farmer cannot promise in which year the rain will be good. A farmer
who depends on the rain can never be **assured** of a harvest. But some
farmers do survive and thrive, season after season — because they
study the climate, choose carefully what to plant, and protect
themselves against the bad years.

Trading is the same. Nobody can promise you a profitable year. What
this program offers is the farmer's discipline applied to markets:

1. **Survive first** — never risk money you need to live.
2. **Validate before you trust** — only strategies with honest,
   out-of-sample evidence.
3. **Then, and only then, try to earn.**

## Paper trading is the default

From the moment you open the application, you are in **Paper Trading
mode**. All money is simulated. Real trading is **disabled** and will
stay that way for a long time — it lives behind multiple safety
gates and cannot be turned on by accident.

You do not need an account on any exchange. You do not need an API
key. You do not need to identify yourself or deposit anything. The
application works fully without credentials.

## Where a complete beginner should begin

Follow this order — each step prepares you for the next:

1. **Open the Learning Center** (button in the main window, or the
   View menu). It works offline and teaches from zero.
2. Start with lesson 1, *"What is cryptocurrency?"* — even if you
   think you already know, the early lessons build the vocabulary the
   later ones depend on.
3. Whenever a new word appears, look it up in the **Glossary**
   (`docs/en/beginners/glossary.md` or from the Learning Center).
4. After the first ten lessons, load a sample CSV of candles and just
   **look at the chart**. Don't trade. Learn to read it.
5. Only after understanding fees, slippage, and risk should you run
   your first backtest — and expect the result to be worse than you
   imagined. That is normal, and it is the tool being honest with you.

## Why you should not use real money while learning

- Most retail traders lose money — commonly reported studies put the
  figure at 70–95%, and most quit within two years.
- They lose not because markets are rigged against them, but because
  they trade without a validated strategy, without risk control, and
  with money they cannot afford to lose.
- Fees, spread, and slippage quietly eat into every trade, and
  beginners rarely count them.
- Simulated practice costs nothing and teaches almost everything a
  beginner needs. There is no reason to pay tuition with real money.

**The single most important rule of this entire program:** never
trade with money you need to live — not your rent, not your food, not
your emergency savings — and never trade with borrowed money. The
farmer who mortgages his farm to buy seed survives one drought less
than the others. If losing it would change your life, it is too much.


## Research tools

You can track your experiments with two tools:

- **Research Notebook**: write down your hypotheses and observations
- **AI Assistant**: get guidance on strategy development and data analysis

These tools help you stay disciplined and learn from each experiment.
